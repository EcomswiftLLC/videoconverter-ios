## App Review notes (2165 of 4000)

Fluxa converts video and audio files between formats on the device. There is no account, no login and no demo credentials. Everything works on first launch.

How to test:
1. On Home, tap Choose a video and pick any video from Photos (or open a file from Files). On iPad you can also drag a file onto the drop zone.

Permissions: Photos (the system photo picker for reading, add-only access to save results) and Tracking (App Tracking Transparency, for ads). The app does not use the camera, the microphone or location.
