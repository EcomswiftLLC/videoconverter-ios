This policy explains what Fluxa does with information. In short: your videos and audio files are converted on your device and are never uploaded, and the only outside party that receives any data is the advertising service that pays for the app.

## 1. Summary

- Fluxa has no account and no sign-in.
- The files you convert are read, converted and saved on your device. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.
- Fluxa is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and ad data to show and measure ads. You are asked for your consent where the law requires it.
- We do not run analytics of our own, and there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
- Fluxa builds no profile of you. Tracking can happen only through the ads: if you allow tracking when iOS asks, Google's advertising software may use your advertising identifier to show and measure ads, and Apple counts that as tracking. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", it does not. See sections 4 and 5.

## 2. Your files stay on your device

You choose which files to convert. Fluxa opens videos through the iOS photo picker, so it does not need access to your whole photo library and sees only the items you pick. You can also open files from the Files app, or drag them in on iPad. When you save a converted video to your photo library, Fluxa asks for add-only permission, which lets it add new items and does not let it read the rest of your library.

All conversion happens on your device: reading the file, changing the container and codecs, extracting audio, making GIFs and animated images, trimming, resizing and the batch queue. Fluxa uses Apple's media frameworks and the FFmpeg libraries, both running inside the app on your device. Your videos, audio and file names are not sent to Ecom Swift LLC, to Google or to any server, and they are not used to train any model.

Converted files are written to the app's own storage on your device until you save or share them. Fluxa never overwrites or deletes your original file. When you save or share a result, Fluxa hands the file to your photo library, the Files app or the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice, and we do not receive it.

Your settings (for example your last chosen format and presets) and a short list of recent conversions are stored only on your device.

## 3. Information we receive

**From the app:** none. We do not receive your files, your identity or how you use the app.

**From you, if you write to us:** if you email support@ecomswiftllc.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the request. We keep support emails as long as needed to help you and to meet our legal duties, and then delete them. Please do not send us video files unless we ask for one to look into a problem.

**From the website:** the Fluxa website (ecomswiftllc.github.io/fluxa/) is a set of static pages. It uses no cookies of its own, no analytics scripts, no advertising scripts and no third-party fonts. Our web host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and operation.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fluxa is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows a banner ad on the Home screen and, before you save or share a converted file, offers one rewarded video ad that you choose to watch. There are no other ads, and no ad covers the conversion controls.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and use, according to Google:

- device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, and other device and app identifiers
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
- information about the ads you see and interact with
- device, operating system and app information, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

We do not receive this data and we do not combine it with anything we hold. Google's use of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on the page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Some US state privacy laws treat advertising of this kind as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". Fluxa does not sell your files. You can limit ad personalisation as described in the next section.

## 5. Your choices about ads and tracking

- **Consent form (EEA, UK and other regions where required).** When you first open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform may appear. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. You can open it again later in Settings, in the Ad privacy choices row, where one is required.
- **Tracking prompt.** After the consent step, iOS may ask whether Fluxa may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and the ads you see are less personalised. The app works the same either way.
- **iOS settings.** You can change the tracking choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier in the same area.
- **Ads cannot be switched off inside the app**, because ads are how Fluxa is paid for. If you decline a consent form that applies in your region, ads may not load for you. In that case no banner appears, and your exports go ahead without an ad.
- **Report an ad.** If you see an ad that is offensive, misleading or inappropriate, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

## 6. Rewarded video and exports

A rewarded video is shown only if you choose to watch it ("Watch a short ad to export"). One watched ad covers the export it unlocks, including a whole batch, and any further exports for 2 minutes after it finishes. If you choose Not now, nothing is saved or shared and you can try again at any time. If no ad is available (for example when you are offline, when no ad can be shown, or when your consent choice does not allow ads), the export goes ahead without one. The app remembers that an ad was watched only on your device, for that short time. This record contains no personal information and is not sent anywhere.

## 7. Deleting your data

- Fluxa has no account. Deleting the app removes everything the app stores, including converted files you have not saved and your recent list.
- Inside the app you can clear the recent list and remove converted files you no longer need.
- Videos you saved to your photo library, and files you exported or shared, are outside the app. You manage and delete them where you saved or sent them.
- Your device backup (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may contain a copy of the app's data according to your Apple settings. That copy is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.
- We hold no personal data about you from your use of the app, so there is nothing on our side to delete. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's controls (https://myadcenter.google.com) or contact Google.

## 8. Children

Fluxa is a general file conversion tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not designed for the Kids category and does not use child-directed ad treatment. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will delete it.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information from your use of the app, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can write to support@ecomswiftllc.com about any request and we will respond within the time the law requires. For data held by Google, exercise your rights with Google. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 10. Security

Your files are protected by the security of your device and by iOS app storage protections. We recommend a device passcode. No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for the files you export and share.

## 11. International transfers

Google and its partners may process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on the legal transfer mechanisms described in Google's policies.
