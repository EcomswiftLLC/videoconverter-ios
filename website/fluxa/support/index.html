Common questions

How do I convert a video to MP4?
  1. On the Home screen, choose a video from Photos or Files. On iPad you can also drag a file in.
  2. Tap MP4 in the format grid, or the Universal MP4 preset.
  3. Tap Convert and wait for the progress to finish.
  4. Tap Save or Share.
How do I extract the audio from a video?

Choose the video, then pick MP3, M4A, WAV or another format in the Audio group of the format grid, or use the Audio only preset. Fluxa saves only the sound.

A file will not open or convert

Fluxa shows the reason. The most common ones:

  • Protected file: films and shows bought or rented from a store, or downloaded from a streaming service, use DRM. These cannot be converted.
  • Encrypted file: password-protected files cannot be read.
  • Damaged or incomplete file: try copying it to your device again.
  • Rare codec: some very old or unusual codecs are not supported. Tell us the format and we will look at it.
Why is my converted file larger or smaller than the original?

File size depends on the codec, the resolution and the quality you choose. Lossless formats such as WAV, FLAC and ProRes are large. Re-wrapping (for example MKV with H.264 to MP4) keeps almost the same size. Choose a lower resolution or quality in Advanced for a smaller file.

Why does HEVC not appear on my device?

Saving as HEVC uses the hardware encoder in your device, which some older iPhone and iPad models do not have. Choose H.264, which plays on almost everything.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Fluxa is free, and ads pay for it. When you save or share, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers that export, even a whole batch, and further exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is saved. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the export goes ahead without one.

My conversion is slow or stops

Re-encoding long or 4K videos takes time and battery, especially on older devices. Keep Fluxa open until the conversion finishes, make sure there is free storage, and try a lower resolution. Re-wrapping is much faster than re-encoding, so pick a format that can hold the original codecs when you can.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Write to us at the address above. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open Settings in the app and look for the Ad privacy choices row, which appears where the law requires it. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.

Where can I get the FFmpeg source code?

See the open-source licences page. It links the build scripts and the exact source, and explains how to ask us for a copy.
