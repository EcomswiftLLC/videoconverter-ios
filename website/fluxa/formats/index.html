Supported formats

What Fluxa opens, what it saves, and what it cannot do. Codecs are listed best first; the first one is the default.

Formats it saves

When the source already uses codecs the new format can hold, Fluxa re-wraps the file without re-encoding: it takes seconds and nothing is lost. H.264 and HEVC use the device's hardware encoder.

Formats it opens

Apple formats are read by iOS itself; the rest are read with the FFmpeg libraries inside the app. Both run on your device.

Apple video

MP4, MOV, M4V, 3GP, 3G2, QT, HEVC and HEIC video, ProRes, Live Photo video, slow motion and Cinematic clips

Open and web video

MKV, WebM, OGV, OGG and OGM video, AV1, VP8 and VP9, GIF, animated WebP, APNG

Windows and legacy video

AVI (including DivX and XviD), WMV, ASF, FLV, F4V, RM, RMVB

Camera, DVD and broadcast

MPG, MPEG, MPE, M2V, VOB, TS, M2TS, MTS (AVCHD), MXF, DV, MJPEG

Audio

MP3, AAC, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, OGG, OGA, Opus, WMA, AMR, AC3, EAC3, MKA

What it cannot convert

DRM-protected files, such as films and shows bought or rented from a store or downloaded from a streaming service. Fluxa does not remove copy protection.

Encrypted or password-protected files.

Some very old or rare codecs, and damaged files whose data cannot be read.

HEVC output on devices without a hardware HEVC encoder. Choose H.264 instead.

Some formats cannot hold everything the source has: a GIF has no sound and at most 256 colours per frame, and some formats cannot carry subtitles or more than one audio track. Fluxa shows a clear message when a file cannot be converted, instead of failing quietly. If a file you think should work does not, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and tell us the format.
