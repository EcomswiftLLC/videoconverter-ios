Open-source licences

Fluxa is built on open-source software. This page lists it, explains your rights under its licences, and tells you how to get the source code.

FFmpeg, under the LGPL

Fluxa uses libraries from the FFmpeg project (ffmpeg.org), licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later (LGPL). They are built without the GPL and non-free options, so no part of FFmpeg in Fluxa is under the GPL.

  • The FFmpeg libraries are included as a separate, dynamically linked framework (FFmpegLGPL.framework) inside the app. You may replace it with a modified version of the same libraries.
  • No changes were made to the FFmpeg source code. The exact versions and the build script are published in the public repository github.com/EcomswiftLLC/ffmpeg-ios-lgpl (see scripts/build.sh and scripts/versions.env), and the FFmpeg source releases are at ffmpeg.org/releases.
  • Nothing in Fluxa's terms of use limits your rights under the LGPL, including modifying the libraries and reverse engineering the app to debug such modifications.
  • The full licence text is at gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html.

Written offer for the source code

For at least three years from the date you received a version of Fluxa, Ecom Swift LLC will give anyone who asks a complete copy of the corresponding source code of the LGPL libraries in that version, including the build scripts, for no more than the cost of providing it (usually nothing, as a download link). Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and tell us your Fluxa version, which you can see in Settings.
