Your files never leave your device

Fluxa has no account and no server that stores your files. Every conversion runs on your iPhone or iPad, with Apple's media frameworks and the open-source FFmpeg libraries. We run no analytics of our own.

  • The iOS photo picker shows Fluxa only the videos you choose
  • Saving to your library uses add-only permission
  • Your original file is never overwritten
  • No account, no sign-in, no subscription

The only outside service is the ad network. The privacy policy explains what it collects.

Free, and paid for by ads

Fluxa has no subscription and no in-app purchase. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it, in two places.

Where you see ads

  • A banner on the Home screen, never over the conversion controls.
  • One short video, which you choose to watch, when you save or share. It covers the whole export, even a batch, and further exports for 2 minutes.

When no ad is available

  • Your export goes ahead for free.
  • This happens, for example, when you are offline.

Where the law requires it, the app shows a consent form from Google before personalised ads, and iOS may ask whether the app may track you. You can say no and still use everything.

What it cannot do

Fluxa cannot convert DRM-protected files, such as films bought or rented from a store or downloaded from a streaming service, or encrypted files. Some very old or rare codecs may not open. Saving as HEVC needs a device with a hardware HEVC encoder.

When a file cannot be converted, Fluxa says so and explains why.

Questions

Is Fluxa really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no paid version and no watermark. Ads from Google AdMob pay for the app: a banner on the Home screen, and one rewarded video that you choose to watch before you save or share.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Fluxa converts every file on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive your videos, your audio or your file names.

Why is MKV to MP4 so fast sometimes?

Many MKV files already hold H.264 or HEVC video and AAC audio, which MP4 can hold too. Then Fluxa only moves the streams into the new container instead of re-encoding them. It takes seconds and the picture and sound are exactly the same.

Which formats can it open and save?

It opens MKV, AVI, WebM, WMV, FLV, MOV, MP4, MTS, VOB and many more, and saves 12 video formats, 9 audio formats and 3 animated image formats. The formats page has the full list with codecs.

Why can't it convert a film I bought?

Films and shows bought or rented from a store, or downloaded from a streaming service, are protected by DRM. Fluxa cannot read protected or encrypted files, and it tells you so instead of failing quietly.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

When you save or share, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers that export, even a whole batch, and further exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is saved and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the export goes ahead anyway. Choosing formats and converting never needs an ad.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no sign-up.

Does it need access to my photo library?

Not to open files. You pick videos with the iOS photo picker, and Fluxa sees only the ones you choose. To save a result, the app asks for add-only permission, which lets it add items and does not let it read your library. Files from the Files app need no permission.

What happens if I do not allow tracking?

The app works the same and the ads are less personalised. You can change the choice any time in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking. Fluxa runs no analytics of its own.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, in portrait and landscape. Saving as HEVC uses the hardware encoder, which some older devices do not have; choose H.264 instead.
